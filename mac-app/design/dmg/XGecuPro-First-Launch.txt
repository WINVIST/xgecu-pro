XGecu Pro 1.2.0-beta.1 - first launch on macOS

1. Download only from https://github.com/WINVIST/xgecu-pro/releases/tag/v1.2.0-beta.1
   and verify the matching SHA-256 file before opening the download.
   From the folder containing both files, run:

   shasum -a 256 -c XGecuPro-macOS-arm64.dmg.sha256

   For the ZIP alternative:
   shasum -a 256 -c XGecuPro-macOS-arm64.zip.sha256

   Stop if the checksum does not report OK.

2. Open the verified DMG and drag XGecuPro to Applications. Wait for copying
   to finish and eject the volume. For the ZIP, extract and copy the app.

3. Try opening the installed app from Applications.

This build has an ad hoc signature but no Apple Developer ID or notarization.
If macOS blocks it, Apple's normal approval route is System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Open Anyway after the first launch attempt:
https://support.apple.com/102445

If macOS reports a damaged app, and the checksum passed, verify the installed
app's signature first. On your own unmanaged Mac, if you trust this exact
release, the following chained commands remove this app's quarantine only
after its signature verification succeeds, then open the app:

codesign --verify --deep --strict /Applications/XGecuPro.app &&
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/XGecuPro.app &&
open /Applications/XGecuPro.app

Paste all three lines together into Terminal. Stop if signature verification
fails. The commands do not require sudo and do not add Apple notarization.
On a managed Mac, follow the administrator's policy if it prevents approval.
